About this cube

This is the VTES Italy Draft Cube: the card pool the Italian community uses to play Vampire: The Eternal Struggle in draft format. Players build their decks from cards picked out of the cube instead of bringing their own.

The crypt has 120 vampires from nine clans, two copies each. In the library, commons usually come in four copies, uncommons in two and rares in one. The badge on each card shows how many copies are in the cube.

Many library cards carry a DRAFT clause: an extra effect that applies only in draft play, often the same effect with a different discipline or clan. “Above” refers to the text printed on the card shown here, which can differ from the current rules text. Disciplines keep their printed abbreviations: [tha] is Blood Sorcery (formerly Thaumaturgy) and [obt] is Oblivion (formerly Obtenebration).
